# Weekly groceries

What I buy every week, what I buy now and then, and where to buy it. This is my real
list; the [recipe catalog's shopping list](../recipe-catalog/ingredients/shopping-list.md)
is a researched plan for cooking its menu.

## Now and then

- Tomatoes, peppers (romano or bell) and onions
- Plain unsweetened wholegrain cereal (Waitrose's so far; haven't tried many others)
- Tuna in olive oil
- Tofu, the XXL pack from the standard brand sold across the UK
