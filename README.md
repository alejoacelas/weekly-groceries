# Weekly groceries

What I buy every week, what I buy now and then, and where to buy it. This is my real
list; the [recipe catalog's shopping list](../recipe-catalog/ingredients/shopping-list.md)
is a researched plan for cooking its menu.

## Now and then

- Tomatoes, peppers (romano or bell) and onions
- Plain unsweetened wholegrain cereal (Waitrose's so far; haven't tried many others)
- Tuna in olive oil
- Tofu, the XXL pack from the standard brand sold across the UK

## Where I buy it

The weekly staples come in one Waitrose home delivery, with one loaf in each order.
I buy fresh bread myself on the other days.

Waitrose requires a [£40 minimum per order](https://www.waitrose.com/ecom/help-information/customer-service/delivery-pass),
counted after discounts. Delivery slots cost £2–4 each, or nothing with a
[Delivery Pass](https://www.waitrose.com/ecom/delivery-pass) (£85 a year).

## Open questions

1. **Protein.** Trying frozen chicken breast as the everyday protein and salmon once
   or twice a week, chosen so nothing spoils. At Waitrose, chicken breast costs about
   £1 per 30g of protein, and salmon or mackerel about £3.
